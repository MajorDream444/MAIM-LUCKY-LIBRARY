# MAIM Lucky Library

**A living, collectible learning universe built around Lucky's global journey and the MAIM mindset.**

The MAIM Lucky Library combines story, history, global citizenship, AI literacy, self-knowledge, problem-solving, physical books, workbooks, audio, video, classroom materials, and a long-running treasure hunt.

Lucky is not a perfect hero. He gets distracted, makes assumptions, follows the wrong people, forgets what his body needs, misreads situations, loses things, and sometimes searches for treasure that was already within reach. Each adventure helps him develop a tool for navigating life.

## The Core Collection

The primary collection contains 27 volumes:

- 26 standalone books, one for each letter of the alphabet
- Book 27, **Lucky's Atlas of Hidden Treasure**, which combines clues from the full collection
- The first commercial release is the ABC set: Awareness, Belief, and Context

Every volume includes:

- A global story
- A practical MAIM principle
- Multiple age pathways
- Applications across life, relationships, school, sports, business, technology, and the world
- A workbook quest
- A conversation with Major Dream Williams
- A hidden clue connected to the larger universe

## Knowledge Layers

1. **Canonical Library** - The 27-book Lucky universe.
2. **Major's Reading Room** - Books, ideas, histories, and thinkers that influence Major's questions and worldview.
3. **Living World Research** - Dated weekly signals from educators, young people, parents, creators, museums, communities, and emerging technology.
4. **Learning Paths** - Personalized routes shaped by age, goals, interests, confidence, pace, access, and demonstrated understanding.
5. **Collector World** - Artifacts, maps, codes, physical editions, bundles, and the final treasure hunt.

## Repository Principles

- MAIM means Major AI Mindset.
- The learner develops their own judgment. The curriculum does not demand ideological agreement.
- Global stories require local context and cultural review.
- Current events may inspire stories, but the lesson must remain evergreen.
- AI supports human agency; it does not replace human wisdom.
- Child safety, privacy, accessibility, and inclusion are design requirements.
- Source PDFs and copyrighted books are not stored publicly without permission.
- Research is evidence, not automatic canon.
- No weekly research result enters a manuscript without editorial review.
