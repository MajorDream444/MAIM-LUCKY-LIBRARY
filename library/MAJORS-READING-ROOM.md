# Major's Reading Room

## Purpose

Major's Reading Room contains books, thinkers, histories, strategies, and arguments that influence the questions behind the MAIM Lucky Library.

It is not a list of doctrines that MAIM endorses. Sources may conflict with one another. Their value often lies in comparison:

- Freedom versus authority
- Discipline versus autonomy
- Law versus virtue
- Individual sovereignty versus social responsibility
- Surveillance versus safety
- Strategy versus ethics
- Efficiency versus humanity
- Leadership by force versus leadership by service

The curriculum should help learners compare these tensions and examine consequences.

## Editorial Method

Every source note should separate:

1. What the author argues
2. Historical context
3. Evidence or examples used
4. Strongest insight
5. Strongest criticism
6. What MAIM may adapt
7. What MAIM should reject or challenge
8. Age suitability
9. Geographic and cultural review
10. Possible Lucky story seed

## Storage Policy

This public repository stores original summaries, bibliographic metadata, limited quotations when justified, and MAIM analysis.

It does not store commercial PDFs, pirated books, or full copyrighted manuscripts without permission.
