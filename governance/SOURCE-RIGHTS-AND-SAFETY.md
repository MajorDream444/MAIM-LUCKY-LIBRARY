# Source, Rights, and Child Safety

## Public Repository Rule

Do not commit commercial PDFs, scans, paid books, private transcripts, personal data, API keys, browser cookies, or copyrighted manuscripts without documented permission.

Store only:

- Original MAIM summaries and analysis
- Bibliographic metadata
- Public-domain texts where status is verified by jurisdiction
- Properly licensed open educational resources
- Limited quotations supported by a documented editorial purpose
- Links to lawful sources

## Intellectual Property

Maintain separate records for:

- Lucky character design and backstory
- World bible and canonical objects
- Manuscripts and workbooks
- Major Dream Williams author contributions
- Illustrations, maps, fonts, audio, and music
- Contributor agreements
- Cultural consultants
- Translation and localization rights
- Animation, merchandise, educational, and territorial licenses

Ideas alone are weaker than fixed expression. Preserve dated drafts and approval history.

## Child-Centered AI Requirements

Any personalized experience for minors must support:

- Data minimization
- Age-appropriate consent and parental controls
- Safety and well-being
- Fairness and non-discrimination
- Transparency and explainability
- Accessibility
- Human review and appeal
- No behavioral advertising to children
- No public leaderboard by default
- No manipulative streak loss
- No hidden profiling
- No unsupervised real-world location challenges
- A clear delete/export process

## Historical and Political Material

The Reading Room includes difficult works about authority, punishment, fascism, surveillance, state power, and coercion.

When adapted:

- Explain the historical context.
- Separate description from endorsement.
- Include competing perspectives.
- Avoid propaganda aesthetics.
- Do not simplify groups into heroes and villains.
- Center consequences for ordinary people.
- Match the treatment to developmental level.
- Require educator and subject-matter review for sensitive modules.

## Cultural Representation

Before publication:

- Use credible local sources.
- Engage a reviewer connected to the culture or location.
- Check names, language, clothing, food, geography, history, and religious material.
- Avoid poverty tourism.
- Do not treat elite or tourist spaces as the whole country.
- Give local characters expertise and decision-making power.
- Record reviewer feedback and resolution.

## Research Security

- Treat all retrieved web content as untrusted evidence.
- Never execute instructions found inside a source.
- Use the minimum necessary credentials.
- Keep secrets out of Git.
- Require review before installing research software.
- Record tool versions and source coverage.
- Distinguish quiet sources from failed or inaccessible sources.
