# Major Dream Williams - Author Voice Protocol

## Purpose

Every Lucky volume includes an original contribution from Major Dream Williams. This is not a generic foreword or an AI-generated imitation. It is a documented conversation drawn from Major's actual words, memories, decisions, mistakes, travel, relationships, and lived experience.

## Required Author Intake for Each Letter

Before a manuscript is finalized, record a conversation with Major covering:

1. What does this letter mean to you?
2. When did you misunderstand it?
3. What life event taught it to you?
4. Where were you, who was present, and what was at stake?
5. How did your South Bronx, Mount Vernon, Jamaica, finance, athletics, travel, building, or entrepreneurial experiences affect your interpretation?
6. What do children usually get told about this idea that you disagree with?
7. What would you tell your younger self?
8. How does the principle apply to AI?
9. What danger appears when the principle is overused or misunderstood?
10. What question should the reader carry into the world?

## Canonical Book Elements

Each volume should contain:

- **A Word from Major** - 300-700 original words
- **Major's Field Note** - one concrete lived anecdote
- **The Question I Still Carry** - intellectual humility
- **Try This Today** - a practical action
- **You Are the Wisdom. AI Is the Intelligence.** - used only where earned

## Voice Standards

Major's voice should remain:

- Direct but not preachy
- Curious rather than falsely certain
- Culturally grounded without caricature
- Comfortable with contradiction
- Honest about mistakes
- Able to speak to a child and an adult simultaneously
- Focused on expanding the reader's perspective
- Oriented toward agency, legacy, and practical action

## Authenticity Controls

- Preserve recordings or transcripts privately with consent.
- Mark direct quotations separately from edited narration.
- Major approves every author section before publication.
- AI may organize and edit, but may not invent biography.
- If a memory is uncertain, label it for verification.
- Historical claims inside anecdotes require sources.
- Public name: Major Dream Williams.
- Legal or contracting identity is handled outside reader-facing narrative unless intentionally relevant.

## Intellectual Property Record

For each author contribution, record:

- Intake date
- Source recording or transcript ID
- Editor
- Draft version
- Major's approval date
- Approved quotations
- Rights or third-party names requiring clearance
