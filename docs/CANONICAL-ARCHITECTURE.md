# MAIM Lucky Library - Canonical Architecture

## Core Promise

Lucky helps people learn how to see, think, decide, build, and live in a changing world.

The universe connects timeless human patterns with modern tools. A story may be inspired by a recent event, but the lasting lesson concerns attention, power, cooperation, courage, identity, fairness, responsibility, resilience, or another durable human challenge.

## Audience Paths

| Path | Approximate age | Primary experience |
|---|---:|---|
| Lucky Explorers | 6-10 | Illustrated story, observation, drawing, read-aloud conversation |
| Lucky Navigators | 11-17 | Identity, friendship, school, sports, media, AI, early enterprise |
| Lucky Captains | 18-26 | Career, business, money, leadership, systems, relationships, responsibility |
| Crew Guides | Adults | Parent, coach, teacher, librarian, mentor, facilitator |

One central story serves all paths. Activities and interpretation deepen by level.

## Seven Application Lenses

Every volume should demonstrate its principle through:

1. Self, body, and emotion
2. Family, friendship, and community
3. School and learning
4. Sports and performance
5. Business, money, and creation
6. Technology, AI, and media
7. History, culture, and the wider world

## Standard Story Rhythm

1. New port or place
2. Life trap or loop
3. Lucky's first assumption
4. Missing context
5. MAIM tool introduced
6. Decision under pressure
7. Consequences
8. Reflection with Major
9. Reader quest
10. Hidden clue
11. Next horizon

## Personalized Learning

The digital experience may personalize:

- Reading level and language
- Audio versus text preference
- Interests: sports, art, science, business, history, technology
- Confidence and support needs
- Quest difficulty
- Pace and reminders
- Examples and practice contexts
- Recommended next volume
- Review intervals based on demonstrated understanding

Personalization must not label a child permanently. Paths should remain explainable, editable, privacy-conscious, and open to exploration.

## Streaks Without Shame

A Duolingo-style continuity system can reinforce practice, but it must not punish life circumstances.

Recommended mechanics:

- Daily Compass: one small reflection or observation
- Weekly Voyage: one practical quest
- Streak Freeze: built-in rest and recovery
- Crew Streaks: families, classrooms, or teams learn together
- Return Bonus: reward coming back after absence
- No public ranking by default for minors
- Celebrate consistency, curiosity, kindness, and recovery - not only speed

## The 27th Book

**Lucky's Atlas of Hidden Treasure** is the capstone, not a 27th alphabet letter.

Each volume supplies one fragment of the final solution. The final book should still be purchasable and enjoyable alone, but collectors receive a deeper puzzle and alternative ending path.

## Global Story Rules

- A country is never just scenery.
- A local character must possess knowledge and agency.
- Separate verified history, living culture, folklore, and fiction.
- Avoid treating Africa, the Caribbean, Asia, Europe, the Americas, the Pacific, or the Middle East as culturally uniform.
- Include museums, libraries, monuments, neighborhoods, markets, workshops, farms, ports, and ordinary homes.
- Show wealth and poverty without romanticizing or dehumanizing either.
- Use local reviewers for material claims and representation.
- Teach why conditions exist, not merely what a place looks like.

## Curriculum Alignment

The curriculum should be mapped over time to:

- Human-centered AI literacy
- AI ethics, techniques, application, and creation
- Global citizenship
- Social and emotional learning
- Media and information literacy
- Historical thinking
- Financial and entrepreneurial literacy
- Leadership and teamwork
- Sports and performance psychology
- Creative problem-solving

Alignment supports school adoption, but the stories must remain emotionally alive and enjoyable outside formal education.

## Canon Layers

| Layer | Can change? | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Core canon | Rarely | Lucky's identity, central objects, world rules |
| Book canon | Through version control | Plot, destination, characters, clue |
| Learning canon | With evidence | Objectives, activities, assessment |
| Living signals | Weekly | Trends, conversations, current events |
| Experimental | Freely | Prototype quests, covers, activities |

Never allow a weekly trend to silently rewrite core canon.
